Generated by `python3 tools/export_value_catalog.py --format markdown`.

Engine reference: 328/328 labeled field occurrences mapped; 56 blank placeholders excluded. Repeated fields are alternate providers, not extra values.

Mappings are documented in the supplied references, not independently validated on every ECU. Unknown formulas still yield invalid samples. Identification entries expose individual decoded fields; they do not assemble a complete VIN. Unconfirmed extra fields require explicit opt-in.

`reported` means the reference does not fix a unit: the sample retains the ECU-decoded unit. Status fields preserve numeric codes or text; bitfields preserve masked bits without inventing their meanings.

`estimated` providers require `allow_estimated: true`; `unverified` providers require `allow_unverified: true`.
